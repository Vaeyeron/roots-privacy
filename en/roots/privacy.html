Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Applies to the game Roots for Android and iOS and to its web version.

In short

The game asks for no registration, creates no accounts and sends the developer nothing about you. All progress is stored on your device and stays there. The only thing that leaves it are requests made by the advertising network — and only in the versions that have advertising.

What data is processed

Game data

Progress, settings and collected items are stored on the device only, in the app's local storage. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else, and they are gone when the app is deleted. You can move them to another device by hand, from the “Save data” screen in the menu, which hands you the progress as a string; what you do with that string is up to you.

Advertising

From version 6.6 the game shows advertising: a rewarded video (revive the root, double the minerals), an ad between runs and a banner in the main menu. It is served by the Yandex Advertising Network. To select an ad, its component inside the app receives and processes:

  • the device advertising identifier (Advertising ID) — you can reset it or opt out in the device settings;
  • the IP address and the approximate region derived from it;
  • technical details: device model, operating system version, language, app version, screen parameters;
  • impression data: which ad was shown, whether it was tapped, whether a video was watched.

The ad network processes this data as an independent controller under its own rules. The developer does not receive it and sees only aggregate statistics — impression and payout counts.

Yandex Advertising Network data rules: yandex.ru/legal/confidential

Versions without advertising (the web version and builds before 6.6) send nothing at all and hold no internet permission.

What the app does not do

  • it does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or files;
  • it does not determine precise location and does not use GPS;
  • it keeps no accounts and does not tie the game to your identity;
  • it sends your game progress neither to the developer nor to the ad network;
  • it sells data to no one.

Permissions

All of them are visible in the app's permission list:

PermissionWhy
Vibration (Android)short feedback on a vein, a find and the root drying out
Internet and network stateonly to load ads; the game itself works offline
Advertising identifierad selection and fraud protection on the network's side
Add to Photos (iOS)saving a herbarium sheet — only when you press “save”

On Android the herbarium sheet is saved through the system image store, which needs no permission at all. Two further permissions come from the ad library and are not used by the app itself: access to the store service that reports the install source, and an internal permission for in-app broadcasts.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, write to the address below and we will act on it.

Your choices

  • to erase all game data, delete the app — the developer holds no copies;
  • to reset the advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation, use the device settings;
  • to obtain your game data, use the “Save data” screen, which hands it to you as a string;
  • to ask a question about data processing, use the contact address.

Changes

If the set of processed data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change with it. Earlier versions are kept by the developer and are available on request.
